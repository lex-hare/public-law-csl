# 《公法研究》 (Public Law) Citation Style Language

This repository provides a Citation Style Language (CSL) style based on the submission guidelines of *Public Law*, adopted by the Taiwan Society for Environmental and Energy Law on December 30, 2022. The repository currently contains only `public-law.csl`. 本專案提供依據臺灣環境資源與能源法學會於 2022 年 12 月 30 日通過之《公法研究》撰稿凡例製作的 Citation Style Language（CSL）格式檔案，即本專案中唯一的 `public-law.csl` 檔案。

The style is derived from a CSL developed by Mr. Chao Cheng-hao for *National Taiwan University Law Journal* with his allowance. That source style was based on the revised *National Taiwan University Law Journal* formatting template adopted in 2020 and also supports the English citation style in *The Bluebook*. 本格式檔案係經趙承灝先生同意，以其所編定之《臺大法學論叢》CSL 為底稿製作。該底稿係依 2020 年修正通過之〈《臺大法學論叢》（新修）格式範本〉編定，並支援英文 Bluebook 引用格式。

> 趙承灝，〈[「國立臺灣大學法學論叢（2020年新修版）」 CSL](https://www.threads.net/@cheng.hao.ty/post/DDW2ZK-PAeG)〉，最後瀏覽日：2025/03/25。

This `public-law.csl` is not an official CSL style published by the [Taiwan Association for Environment, Resources and Energy Law](https://www.facebook.com/TAIWAN.AEREL) (TAEREL) or *Public Law Review*. It is likewise not an official style published by National Taiwan University College of Law or *National Taiwan University Law Journal*. It is an unofficial implementation based on the publicly available submission guidelines and the aforementioned CSL source. 本專案所提供之 `public-law.csl` 並非臺灣環境資源與能源法學會或《公法研究》官方發布之格式檔案，亦非國立臺灣大學法律學院或《臺大法學論叢》官方發布之格式檔案。本檔案係依公開之撰稿凡例及既有 CSL 底稿製作之非官方實作。

The use of this style is subject to the licensing conditions of its source style. As the CSL released by Mr. Chao permits non-commercial use only, `public-law.csl` is likewise intended for non-commercial use only. 本格式檔案之使用受原底稿之授權條件限制。趙承灝先生所釋出之 CSL 僅允許非營利使用，因此本專案之 `public-law.csl` 同樣僅供非營利使用。
